# TV Wall privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TV Wall does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.

- Your rules and today's usage count are stored on the TV itself, in the app's private storage. They never leave the device.
- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot make network requests. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no servers.
- TV Wall uses Android's accessibility service only to know when the screen is on, to keep the block screen in front once your time is up, and to put the TV in standby. It does not read the content of other apps' screens: `canRetrieveWindowContent` is off in its service configuration.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its stored data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/iBala/tvwall/issues.
